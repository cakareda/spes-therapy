Booking & Payment Policy

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Sessions can be cancelled or rescheduled up to 24 hours in advance. Sessions cancelled or missed with less than 24 hours’ notice may be charged at the full session fee.

Payment

To secure a session, payment must be completed at least 24 hours before the scheduled time. Sessions not paid for by this time may be released to other clients.

No-Shows

If a client does not attend a scheduled session without prior notice, the session may be charged in full.
