About Me

I am Ece Cakar, a licensed psychologist offering online therapy in English and Turkish. I work with adults seeking support for emotional well-being, personal growth, and navigating life’s challenges.

I completed my bachelor’s degree in Psychology at Istanbul Bilgi University, including a year-long exchange program at Philipps University of Marburg in Germany. I later obtained my Master’s degree in Clinical Psychology from Erasmus University Rotterdam in the Netherlands.

My therapeutic approach is grounded in evidence-based practices, including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) and Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR). I tailor therapy to each individual, focusing on both understanding underlying patterns and developing practical tools that support lasting change.

Since 2019, I have worked with individuals from diverse cultural backgrounds, including extensive experience in intercultural and expat settings. This has shaped a sensitive and collaborative therapeutic style that respects personal values, cultural context and individual pace.

Services

Individual Therapy

I offer online individual therapy for adults seeking support in English or Turkish.

Some areas I commonly work with include:

  • Anxiety and stress
  • Low mood and recurring negative thought patterns
  • Burnout and work-related stress
  • Life transitions and adjustment
  • Self-esteem, self-care, and boundary setting
  • Interpersonal and relational difficulties
  • Values clarification

Individual Therapy (45-50-minute individual sessions, 2026 rate): €90 per session

Corporate Support

I also work with organizations and companies to provide psychological support for employees through individual online therapy sessions (EAP). This service is suitable for organizations seeking to support employee well-being in a professional, confidential and flexible manner.

If you would like to explore how this support could be structured for your organization, please contact me to discuss available options.
